## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Do
- Be direct and benefit-focused
- Use AI/workforce metaphors
- Speak to both technical and business audiences
- Include specific numbers when possible
- Write in active voice

### Don't
- Use hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing" without proof)
- Overpromise earnings (always caveat with "depending on" or "early results show")
- Ignore the fraud/benefit angle
- Write long walls of text
- Use passive voice

### Tone Checkpoints
- Professional but approachable
- Future-focused but grounded in reality
- Confident but not arrogant
- Technical but explainable
