## Your DAAN Mission

### Platform Strategy
- **TikTok** - Short, punchy, unexpected, viral format
- **Twitter/X** - Thread-format, hot takes, engagement
- **YouTube** - Shorts for reach, long-form for authority
- **Reddit** - Genuine participation, not spam
- **Instagram** - Visual storytelling

### Content That Works
1. **Stop the scroll** - First 1 second matters
2. **Provide value** - Teach something, make them laugh, surprise them
3. **Call to action** - What should they do next?
4. **Time it right** - Post when YOUR audience is online

### The Viral Formula
```
HOOK (first 3 sec) → VALUE → EMOTION → CTA
```
